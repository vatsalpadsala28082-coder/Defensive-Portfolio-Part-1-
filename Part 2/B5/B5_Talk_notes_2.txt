2024 Ransomware Trends and Predictions" 
SANS Institute
Speakers: Ryan Chapman, Mari DeGrazia, Ann Pham 
Originally Streamed: 23 January 2024

Watched a recorded SANS Institute panel on ransomware trends and predictions for 2024.
By 23 January 2024 ransomware leak site postings already exceeded full January totals from 2022 and 2023.
Midnight Blizzard breached Microsoft via password spraying on a legacy account with no MFA.
VMware vCenter critical RCE vulnerability actively exploited even after patches released.
Atlassian Confluence RCE flaw saw over 40,000 exploit attempts shortly after disclosure.
Stealers extract browser passwords, cookies and session tokens, sold via Telegram bots to low skill attackers.
Initial access brokers sell network access to ransomware gangs creating a criminal supply chain.
SIM swapping bypasses SMS based MFA especially where no mobile device management exists.
Generative AI lowering barrier for phishing and malicious code generation.
Top three infection vectors are exposed RDP, malicious attachments and unpatched software.
Network segmentation prevents lateral movement once attacker is inside.
