B5 – Talk Notes
UCC Tech Talk Week 8
Attended a UCC tech talk about The Cuckoo's Egg by Cliff Stoll.
The session was framed around black hat vs white hat — the attacker being the black hat and Stoll playing the white hat trying to catch him.
The talk was titled Spy vs Stoll which set up the whole cat and mouse dynamic between Stoll and the KGB-linked hacker.
The Cuckoo's Egg is a real book by Cliff Stoll documenting his experience tracking a hacker through Lawrence Berkeley Lab's Unix systems in 1986.
It all started when Stoll found two sets of records that didn't match — a 75 cent discrepancy in the Unix system accounting logs.
He wrote a localised account script to monitor the system and track the attacker's movements in real time.
He would then compare results from the script against normal activity to identify anything suspicious or unauthorised.
